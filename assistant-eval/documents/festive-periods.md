# Festive periods

How we run the stall around the big holidays. Read this a month before each one.

## Chinese New Year

We close on the first, second and third day of Chinese New Year. We reopen on the fourth day.
Take pre-orders for whole roast chickens from one week before. Write each order in the red book with the name, phone number and pick-up time. Ask for a $10 deposit.
Three days before Chinese New Year, double the chicken order from Ah Huat, to 60 birds a day, and tell him by the week before. Seng Kee should also send 5 kg of extra wings.
Hire one extra helper for the last four days before Chinese New Year. Pay is $120 a day in hongbao.
The hawker centre has its spring cleaning the week before; check the NEA notice for the exact days.

## Hari Raya Puasa

Many regular customers from the nearby offices take leave, so lunch is quieter. Order 20 percent fewer chickens on the day itself and the day after.

## Deepavali

The stall stays open. Kumar takes the day off, so Ah Mei needs help from the boss's nephew at lunch.

## Christmas and New Year's Eve

Offices close early. Expect a smaller lunch and a busier dinner if we open late. We close at 3pm on New Year's Eve.

## National Day

The hawker centre is crowded in the evening because of the fireworks. We stay open until 9pm and order 10 more chickens.

## Notes from past years

### 2023

We took 70 chickens a day for three days before Chinese New Year and still sold out by 1pm. The extra helper was Kumar's cousin.

### 2024

Pre-orders for whole roast chickens reached 45. We closed on the first three days of the new year and reopened on day 4 with a short menu.

### 2025

Ah Huat raised his price by 50 cents a bird in the two weeks before Chinese New Year. Order early to lock in the price.
