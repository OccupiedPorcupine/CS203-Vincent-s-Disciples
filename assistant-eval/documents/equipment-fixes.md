# When equipment breaks

## Rice cooker trips the power
Unplug the rice cooker, then reset the breaker switch in the electrical box next to stall 12. Plug it back in after a minute. If it trips again, use the small spare cooker.

## Chiller not cold
Check the door seal and that nothing blocks the fan at the back. If it is still above 4 degrees after an hour, call Lim Refrigeration at 9555 1212 and move the chicken to the ice box.

## Gas smell
Close the gas valve at the cylinder, put out all flames and do not switch on anything electrical. Call Seng Gas's emergency line, 6123 4567, and tell the hawker centre office.
