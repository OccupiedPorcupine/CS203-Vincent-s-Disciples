# Hygiene routine

## Every day
- Wash hands with soap before handling food and after touching money.
- Red chopping board for raw chicken, green for cooked chicken. Never swap them.
- Check the chiller temperature twice a day, at opening and at 2pm. It must be between 0 and 4 degrees. Write it in the log book.
- Throw away cooked chicken that has been out for more than 4 hours.

## Every week
- Saturday after closing: scrub the chopping boards with bleach and soak the ladles.

## Every month
- Pest control comes on the first Wednesday of the month at 3pm. Clear the cabinets under the counter before they arrive.
- The NEA officer may check the log book, so keep it at the stall.
